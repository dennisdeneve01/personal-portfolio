About me

Who I am

I work as a software engineer at SOLIDitech, where I design, develop and test applications for a range of industries, including finance and retail. The work is built on Java, with MySQL behind it and Vue and TypeScript on the front end.

Before I write any code, I ask how a task fits into the wider system. Work usually reaches me with its requirements already defined, but when a business rule isn't clear I go back to the customer to understand it properly rather than guess. I'd rather invest the time in an efficient, scalable solution than ship a quick patch that someone has to revisit later, and I support the junior developers on my team when they need a hand.

I studied Computer Science at the University of Cape Town. Away from the keyboard, I paint. More on that below.
